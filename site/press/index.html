In one line

US Tin is a free app that teaches you what goes in which bin, five minutes at a time, and answers on the spot.

In a paragraph

Most people want to throw things away correctly and mostly can't: one coffee cup is three materials with three answers, and "plastic" is seven different resin codes. US Tin is a free web app that teaches sorting as a short course laid out like a recycling route, with Tin, a cartoon tin can, as your guide. It also answers in the moment, for 177 items, part by part, with the reason why; finds nearby drop-offs from OpenStreetMap; and lets friends race through timed quizzes with a six-digit game PIN. Photo recognition runs entirely on the phone, and the app has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Facts

NameUS Tin (the mascot is Tin)
PriceFree. No ads, no purchases.
Whereus-tin.org, as a web app for phones, tablets and computers
The course12 units, 53 lessons, generated from the same catalog the lookup uses
The catalog177 household items, broken into parts, each with a bin and a resin code or material stream
RecognitionMobileNet v2 plus a material model trained on 2,527 photos of real waste. Useful answers on held-out photos: 77.1%.
Sourcegithub.com/austnn-xu/ustin
