The short version

US Tin sets no cookies, and there are no analytics or advertising trackers on the website or in the app. The app keeps your progress in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device. The website keeps one small note of your choice in the cookie notice.

What's stored in your browser

NameKindWhat it's forHow long
ustin-progressEssentialYour XP, streak, lessons, coins, Tin's outfit and Arena statsUntil you reset or clear it
ustin-settingsEssentialTheme, daily goal, saved ZIP code or location, tours seen, recent game PINsUntil you reset or clear it
ustin-query-cacheEssentialRecent map results and leaderboards, so they load fast24 hours
sb-…-auth-tokenEssential, only if you join the leaderboardYour anonymous leaderboard sessionUntil you remove yourself or clear it
ustin-site-consentEssentialRemembers your answer to the cookie noticeUntil you clear it
ustin-site-install-tabPreferencesRemembers which device you picked in the install guideUntil you clear it, or turn Preferences off

None of these are sent to us. They're read by the page in your own browser.

Why there's a notice at all

Most sites ask before setting cookies because they track you. We don't, but browser storage is still storage, so we'd rather tell you plainly what's in it. "Essential only" turns off the one preference the website keeps. The app itself can't work without its essential storage: it's where your progress lives.

Change your mind

Or clear this site's data in your browser's settings. That also erases your progress in the app, so only do it if you mean to start over.
