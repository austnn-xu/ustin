The short version

  • US Tin is free. There's no account, nothing to buy, and coins have no money value.
  • The answers are general guidance for the US. Your town's rules come first.
  • Be decent on the leaderboard. Names are rolled, not typed, to keep it that way.
  • Please don't use US Tin to decide how to handle dangerous waste without checking with your local program.

Who we are

US Tin is an independent project that teaches people where things go when they throw them away. In these terms, "US Tin", "we" and "us" mean the people who make it, and "you" means anyone using the website at us-tin.org or the US Tin app. You can reach us through GitHub issues.

Using US Tin

You can use US Tin for free, for your own learning, at home, at school or at work. You don't need an account. By using it, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

If you're under 13, please use US Tin with a parent, guardian or teacher, especially the Arena, where your rolled name and scores can appear on a public leaderboard.

The answers are guidance

Every recycling, compost and drop-off answer in US Tin comes from one catalog of 177 items, written for the United States in general. Recycling programs differ from town to town and change over time. If your city, county or waste hauler says something different, follow them.

Items marked as hazardous (batteries, paint, chemicals, medicines, fire extinguishers and the like) need extra care. US Tin tells you they need a special drop-off; it can't tell you how to handle a particular product safely. Read the label and check with your local household hazardous waste program.

Places in Near me come from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers edit. They can be out of date or wrong. Call ahead before you drive somewhere.

Photo recognition makes mistakes. We publish how often on the home page. Check the item it names before you act on the answer.

The Arena and the leaderboard

If you join the leaderboard, you get a name made of three random words from family-friendly lists, plus your Tin's colour and outfit and your match scores. These are public to other players. You can't type your own name, and that's on purpose.

Please don't try to cheat, overload or break the leaderboard or game PINs, or use them to bother other players. We may remove scores, names or games that look like abuse, without notice.

You can delete everything the leaderboard holds about you at any time: in the app, go to Arena, tap your name, then Remove me from the leaderboard.

Coins and the shop

Coins are earned by answering correctly and spent on things for Tin to wear. They can't be bought, sold, traded or exchanged for money or anything outside the app, and they're stored on your device. If your device's storage is cleared, they're gone, and we can't restore them.

Your progress

Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device, not with us. We can't see them, back them up or move them to a new phone. See the privacy notice for exactly what is stored where.

What you may not do

  • Use US Tin in a way that breaks the law, or that harms other people or the service.
  • Try to get around the app's security, including its content security policy and the leaderboard's checks.
  • Present US Tin, its name, Tin the mascot or its artwork as your own product, or suggest we endorse you.
