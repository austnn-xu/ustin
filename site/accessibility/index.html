Our goal

We aim for the website and the app to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Text and controls are checked for colour contrast by a script on every change to the app (npm run check:contrast).
  • Colour is never the only signal: every bin has a colour, an icon and a name, everywhere.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring.
  • Pictures have text alternatives. The coffee-cup animation on the home page has a written description, and the same steps are written out beside it.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are skipped: the cup is shown already taken apart, and Tin stands still.
  • Both the website and the app follow your device's light or dark setting.
  • Text uses real fonts at sizes you can zoom to 200% without losing content.

What we know isn't right yet

  • Arena matches are timed. There's no setting yet to turn the timer off; practice lessons have no timer at all.
  • The map in Near me is OpenStreetMap's own embed. The list of places beside it has the same information in plain text.
  • Photo recognition needs a camera or a photo. Search and the item list do the same job without one.

Tell us

If something in US Tin is hard to use, we want to know: GitHub issues. Tell us the page or screen, what you were trying to do, and the device and assistive technology you use, if you're happy to.
